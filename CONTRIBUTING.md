# Contributing to The Human Edge

Thanks for your interest in this project!

## How to Contribute

- **Typos & Grammar**: Open a PR with fixes
- **Content Suggestions**: Open an Issue with your idea
- **Translations**: Help improve the Chinese/English versions
- **Case Studies**: Share real-world examples of AI-human collaboration

## Guidelines

- Keep chapters in their respective `/en` or `/zh` folders
- For new concepts, update `glossary.md` with terminology
- Be respectful and constructive

## Code of Conduct

Be kind. This is a space for learning and growing together.
