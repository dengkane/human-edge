# Contributing to The Human Edge

Thanks for your interest in this project!

## How to Contribute

- **Typos & Grammar**: Open a PR with fixes
- **Content Suggestions**: Open an Issue with your idea
- **Translations**: Help improve the Chinese/English versions
- **Sources**: Point out a claim that is wrong, out of date, or unsupported
- **Case Studies**: Share real-world examples of AI-human collaboration

## Guidelines

- Chapters live in `chapters/en/` (the source of truth) and `chapters/zh/` (translated from it)
- **Every `<!-- verified -->` marker must be a source you opened**, not one that merely sounds right —
  see [Factual claims](chapters/en/README.md#factual-claims)
- New concepts go into `glossary.md`, including their approved Chinese term
- Chapter filenames are `ch<NN>-<slug>.md` and are identical in both languages
- The maintainers' own flow, including how a chapter is published, is in
  [WORKFLOW.md](WORKFLOW.md); contributors only need to open a normal PR
- Be respectful and constructive

## Code of Conduct

Be kind. This is a space for learning and growing together.
